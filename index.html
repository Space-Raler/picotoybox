Pico ToyBox

Not connected
Needs Chrome on Android or the Bluefy browser on iPhone. The Pico advertises as “PicoToyBox”.

Which base should the Pico pretend to be?

Pick the base before starting the game. The console sees the Pico unplugged and plugged in again as a different device. Do not switch during a game.

Infinity figures

Pick a 320-byte figure dump (.bin) for a slot. Dumps stay on this phone; the Pico remembers loaded figures across power-ups.
Skylanders and LEGO have their own pages (skylanders.html, lego_dimensions.html): switch the mode here, disconnect, then open that page and Connect.
